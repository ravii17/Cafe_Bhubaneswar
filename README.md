# ☕ Cafe Website Demo

## 📌 Overview

This is a demo website created to give cafe owners a quick preview of how their business can look online. It showcases the design, layout, and key features that can be included in a full production website.

The goal is to help you visualize your cafe’s digital presence before committing to a complete website build.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

* Provide a **quick glance** at a modern cafe website
* Demonstrate **UI/UX design and branding possibilities**
* Help clients understand **what to expect** from a final product
* Serve as a **prototype** for customization based on your cafe

---

## ✨ Features

* 🏠 Attractive homepage with branding
* 📖 Menu section with items and pricing
* 📸 Gallery showcasing ambiance and food
* 📍 Location & contact details
* 📱 Fully responsive design (mobile-friendly)
